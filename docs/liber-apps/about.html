A shared place for your design work.

LIBER is a collection of creative and project collaboration tools from Liber Creative LLC. REVEX connects building models, design decisions, specifications and engineering review in a browser workspace.

Explore the building

Review synced BIM geometry, take measurements, coordinate issues and explore design assets.

Keep decisions together

Organize references, selections and images in Design Book and connect them to project specifications.

Review and visualize

Prepare energy studies and review their evidence. Generate architectural images from the current model view using your connected Google account.

Your Google connection

Google sign-in can identify your LIBER account. Rendering asks separately for Google Cloud access so you can choose an available billing project, check access to Gemini and send the viewport image and rendering prompt to Google. Connecting does not purchase a render or change your LIBER login.

You control which Google project pays for image generation. Google handles API billing, credits and spending settings. Render prices are shown as estimates before you generate an image.

Read how LIBER handles account, project and service data in our Privacy Policy. You can remove Google permissions in your Google account.

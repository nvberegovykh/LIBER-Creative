A considered path to a Jericho Alliance Profile.

Prelaunch · Live identity collection and credential issuance are unavailable while provider setup, privacy governance, and PICT integration are completed.

  1. Understand the covenant

    Read the declaration and membership terms. Joining is voluntary, and leaving must be possible.

  2. Verify through a trusted service

    The proposed provider is Stripe Identity. Its hosted flow checks supported identity documents. Verification outcomes require a server-confirmed result.

  3. Create your avatar

    Choose an original pixel-art identity for the community. An avatar represents you creatively and is distinct from evidence used for identity verification.

  4. Receive your JAP

    After successful checks, receive a random 27-digit reference and a downloadable QR. PICT token issuance requires a configured network, documented contract, and separate consent.

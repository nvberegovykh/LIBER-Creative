OPENSTUDIO GEOMETRY COMPILER v3.3.2 — SERIALIZATION HOTFIX
=========================================================

The repair algorithm itself found and decomposed the five-sided opening correctly.
The failure happened afterward while serializing OS:SubSurface back to OSM.

OS:SubSurface field layout:
0 Handle
1 Name
2 Sub Surface Type
3 Construction
4 Parent Surface
5 Adjacent SubSurface
6 View Factor to Ground
7 Frame and Divider
8 Multiplier
9 Number of Vertices
10+ Vertex coordinates

v3.3.1 incorrectly started repaired coordinates at field 8. v3.3.2 starts them at
field 10 and validates the exact saved file by reparsing it before OpenStudio runs.

The fix is schema-based and independent of names such as op-132.

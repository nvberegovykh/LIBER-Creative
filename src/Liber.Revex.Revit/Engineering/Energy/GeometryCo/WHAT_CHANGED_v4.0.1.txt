OpenStudio Energy Model Geometry Compiler v4.0.1 — FINAL FIX
================================================================

1. Native exit-code-3 diagnosis is no longer discarded.
   Full native logs and the complete staged failure folder are retained.

2. Matched constructions are canonicalized generically.
   Both sides of each matched Surface/SubSurface use the same selected physical
   construction handle. OpenStudio performs its supported internal reversal.
   No op-xxx, su-xxx, construction name, or project name is used in the logic.

3. Mapping confidence is honest.
   Anonymous behavior-profile confidence is separated from the confidence of the
   old source space used to clone direct space objects. A weak source match can no
   longer appear as a perfect 1.0 geometry match.

4. Names are optional evidence, never persistent keys.
   Handles/source IDs and geometry are primary. Learned room-label categories are
   an optional fallback only; missing labels produce geometry matching/review.

5. Failed compilations preserve everything required to repair the next issue:
   staged Baseline/Proposed OSM, native logs, translated IDF, EnergyPlus output,
   interim audit, mappings, and traceback.

WHY v3.2 PASSED A MODEL THAT ENERGYPLUS REJECTED
================================================
The old integrity suite checked OSM-level structure: handles, parent links, pairing,
planarity, and schedule preservation. It did not run the complete OSM -> IDF ->
EnergyPlus geometry path.

The failed Baseline exposed two blind spots:
1. op-132 had five vertices. OpenStudio omitted it during forward translation.
2. Ten matched interior door pairs had physically conflicting constructions.

v3.3 fixes the model before release and then validates the same path used by the
simulation:
- lossless opening decomposition,
- pair-aware construction reconciliation,
- ForwardTranslator error/warning gate,
- EnergyPlus design-day geometry smoke test,
- no output commit unless both Baseline and Proposed pass.
